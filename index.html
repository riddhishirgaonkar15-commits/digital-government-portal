💡 Smart Digital Tips

Remember these important tips when using online government services.

🔐 Protect Your OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with another person.

🌐 Use Official Websites

Check the website address carefully before entering personal details.

📁 Keep Documents Ready

Check the document requirements before starting an application.

💾 Save Reference Numbers

Keep your application or acknowledgement number for future tracking.

📱 Log Out Safely

Always log out after using a shared computer or device.

⚠️ Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links promising guaranteed benefits or payments.

🌱 About This CEP Project

Assisting Citizens in Using Government Digital Portals

This Community Engagement Project aims to help citizens understand and access digital government services. It provides simple instructions, document guidance, digital safety awareness and information about common online services.

The project encourages digital literacy and helps citizens become more confident when navigating online services. It is an educational guide and is not an official government website.
